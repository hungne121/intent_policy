# ManiCast speed check — 2026-11-12

`intent_policy/intent/manicast_intent.py::forecast_xi` returns `[time, horizon, 6 joints, xyz]` **future displacement in meters**. The preview uses horizons `[0.1, ..., 1.0]` seconds. The forecaster does not expose a separate velocity target here. For the wrist, a finite-difference speed estimate is `median(||xi[h+1,wrist]-xi[h,wrist]|| / (t[h+1]-t[h]))` in m/s. This computation uses the ManiCast prediction derived causally from the last 0.4 seconds of observed keypoints. The observed comparator is median speed across recent wrist observations.

The shared `intent_policy/experts/human_paced.py` converts hand speed to a bounded robot travel speed. I2 uses a maximum of 0.34 m/s for horizontal transport, 0.22 m/s for lift and 0.14 m/s near contact; I3 uses at most 0.20 m/s in its paced phases and retains slower fixed vertical-contact phases. These limits are pilot geometry constraints, not trained policy behavior. The speed is selected at each movement stage boundary to prevent mid-stage target-time jumps.

In I2 seed 300100 during gesture (2.6–3.5 s), the predicted speed median was **0.022 m/s**, while measured human wrist speed median was **0.246 m/s**. The median one-second wrist position forecast error was 0.194 m. Centre/right destination seeds 300106/300101 had median one-second errors 0.151/0.107 m. In the centre seed, the early forecast candidate was the wrong plate (plate2 instead of plate1), although the expert corrected its final destination using the observed pointing ray. In I3 nominal seed 0 (0.6–2.2 s), measured wrist speed median was 0.204 m/s versus predicted 0.032 m/s. Thus this checkpoint's forecast velocity **underestimates these retargeted gestures**. Observed motion currently determines most of the speed; ManiCast can still trigger a reversible early pick/route, but these pilots do not establish forecast-velocity accuracy or a learned ACT improvement.

The initial I3 trial with a 0.34 m/s transport cap caused about 6 mm finger/cake overlap and failed the geometry gate. The reduced 0.20 m/s pace plus slower vertical phases passed the gate on the three seed-0 I3 cases. I2 still fails the provisional 2 mm fingertip/cake overlap gate at about 3 mm, despite the slower approach. Keep it rejected for dataset collection pending geometry repair.
